# Privacy Policy for Quote Frame

**Last updated: September 29, 2026**

Quote Frame is a personal, non-commercial application used by its owner to generate quote images and publish them to the owner's Google Photos library.

## Google user data

Quote Frame uses Google Photos API access to:

- upload quote images generated by Quote Frame;
- access Google Photos content created by the application as needed to manage that content; and
- edit application-created Google Photos content as needed for the publishing workflow.

Quote Frame does not use Google Photos access to inspect or manage unrelated photos in the user's library.

## Data sharing

Google user data accessed by Quote Frame is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

## Data storage

OAuth credentials required to access Google Photos are stored privately by the application owner. Credentials and other private configuration are not stored in this public repository.

## Data retention and deletion

Application-created content remains in Google Photos until it is removed by the application owner or by Quote Frame's content-management workflow.

The application owner can revoke Quote Frame's access to their Google account at any time through their Google Account permissions.

## Intended users

Quote Frame is a personal application intended solely for use by its owner. It is not offered as a public service.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
